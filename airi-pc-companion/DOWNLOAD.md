# Airi-PC Companion — Download

## Download

Download **AiriPC-Companion-Setup.exe** from the latest GitHub Release.

## Install

Double-click the installer and complete the installation.

## Connect

Open **Airi-PC Companion** and press **CONNETTI AIRI**.

## Verify

The window should show **Connection: ONLINE**, **Authentication: OK**, and **PC: CONNECTED** once the companion transport is paired and reachable.

## Emergency stop

Press **STOP ALL** in the Companion. This locally disables remote actions until the Companion is re-enabled.
